Complete Movie List

  1. Insterstellar (2014)
    • Director: Christopher Nolan
    • Genre: Sci-fi
    • Why it's essential: This genre-defining movie is visually breathtaking and depicts a story full of love and connection across space and time.
  2. Dead Poets Society (1989)
    • Director: Peter Weir
    • Genre: Drama
    • Why it's essential: A powerful story that teaches you to find your own voice, even when the world pushes back
  3. Little Women (2019)
    • Director: Greta Gerwig
    • Genre: Coming of Age
    • Why it's essential: A story of 4 sisters navigation life, love, loss, and ambition.
  4. Tangled (2010)
    • Director: Byron Howard and Nathan Greno
    • Genre: Animation/Romance
    • Why it's essential: A story showing your life is what you experience.
  5. The Help (2011)
    • Director: Tate Taylor
    • Genre: Drama
    • Why it's essential: A brilliant commentary on class and racial inequality
  6. The Sound of Music (1965)
    • Director: Robert Wise
    • Genre: Musical/ Romance/ Drama/ Family
    • Why it's essential: A timeless classic that combine music, romance, and family value in 1940's Austria.
  7. The Princess Bride (1987)
    • Director: Rob Reiner
    • Genre: Comedy/Adventure/ Fantasy
    • Why it's essential: A genre defining fairytale epic with unforgettable dialogue and lovable characters.
  8. Oceans 11 (2001)
    • Director: Steven Soderbergh
    • Genre: Thriller/Crime
    • Why it's essential: A stylish heist film with a talented ensemble cast
  9. Barry Potter 1 (2001)
    • Director: Chris Columbus
    • Genre: Fantasy/Adventure
    • Why it's essential: A near perfect and magical adaptation of a beloved book series integrating magic and fantasy into a young boy's life.
  10. Ford v. Ferrari (2019)
    • Directors: James Mangold
    • Genre: Drama/Sports
    • Why it's essential: Turns a true story about car racing into a tense character-driven battle of ambition, ego, and friendship that feels human, not mechanical.
